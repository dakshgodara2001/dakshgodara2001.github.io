03. Featured Case Study

INDmoney · Term Life Insurance

How we became India's 3rd largest digital insurance aggregator

Context

INDmoney wanted to expand beyond wealth management into insurance. Term life insurance was chosen as the entry point — high ticket size, high intent, and strong unit economics. There was no existing insurance infrastructure in the product.

Challenge

Insurance is a trust-heavy, regulation-heavy category. Users compare across aggregators before buying. We needed to build the full stack — quote engine, proposal journey, insurer integrations, payment, issuance, and post-sale servicing — while competing with established players like Policybazaar.

Approach

  • Built a modular integration layer to onboard insurers fast — integrated ICICI Prudential, Bajaj Life, Tata AIA, and Axis Max Life
  • Designed the funnel for maximum DIY conversion — minimal steps, smart defaults, real-time premium calculation
  • Built an end-to-end CRM supporting 50+ sales agents for assisted conversions
  • Introduced Riders/Add-ons to increase ATV without adding funnel friction
  • Integrated auto-dialer into CRM, improving call connectivity by 30%

Key Decisions

  • DIY-first, assisted-second: Optimized the funnel so users could complete purchase independently, with sales agents stepping in only when needed — this kept CAC low
  • Speed over coverage: Launched with 4 insurers instead of waiting for 10 — got to market fast and iterated based on user preference data
  • CRM as a product: Built the internal CRM as a full product (not a hack) — this became a platform used across insurance verticals

Results

#3 Largest digital insurance aggregator by revenue in India
Highest Funnel conversion rates in the industry
30% Increase in call connectivity via auto-dialer
50+ Sales agents supported on CRM platform
